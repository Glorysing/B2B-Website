## 关于项目

[nantong-plastic.com](https://nantong-plastic.com) 是广东南彤科技有限公司的 B2B 企业官网，面向全球化妆品、个护品牌提供专业的泵阀包装采购与 OEM/ODM 合作渠道。

网站从零开始定制开发——基于 WordPress 定制主题，不使用任何页面构建器，涵盖产品展示、中英双语、在线询盘等核心业务功能，面向 Google 国际 SEO 深度优化，覆盖北美、欧洲、中东、东南亚、日韩等 **20+ 国家**市场。

## About

[nantong-plastic.com](https://nantong-plastic.com) is the official B2B corporate website for Guangdong Nantong Technology Co., Ltd. — a precision cosmetic pump dispenser manufacturer serving global personal care and beauty brands across OEM/ODM channels.

The site was built as a fully custom WordPress theme from scratch — no page builders, no templates — covering product showcasing, bilingual support, and online inquiry workflows, with deep SEO optimization targeting **20+ international markets** including North America, Europe, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, Japan, and Korea.
