## About

[nantong-plastic.com](https://nantong-plastic.com) is the official B2B corporate website for Guangdong Nantong Technology Co., Ltd. — a precision cosmetic pump dispenser manufacturer established in 2010, serving global personal care and beauty brands across OEM/ODM channels.

Built as a fully custom WordPress theme from scratch with no page builders, covering product showcasing, bilingual support, and online inquiry workflows. Deep international SEO targeting **20+ markets** including North America, Europe, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, Japan, and Korea.
